My path as an engineer started with curiosity about how the web functions at scale — from pixel rendering pipelines to distributed backend architectures. But as I built systems, I realized technical depth is only half the equation.

Operating a licensed MP Online & Citizen Digital Services Center brought me face-to-face with everyday citizens, students, and job aspirants navigating complex government portals and certification workflows. Observing their real-world user friction firsthand inspired me to architect civic tech platforms like SarkareeUpdate and the MP CPCT 2026 Examination Portal.

Across tier-2 and tier-3 cities in India, thousands of eager minds are held back by theoretical, disconnected college syllabi. That conviction led to founding Kurukshetra Coding School in Bhopal. Here, we don't just teach syntax — we train students to think like product architects and build applications that solve immediate regional problems.
